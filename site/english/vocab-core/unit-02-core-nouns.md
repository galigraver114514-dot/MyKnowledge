# Unit 02 · 高频名词 12

> 目标：掌握可数性（可数/不可数）与常用搭配。

| # | 单词 | 可数 | 核心义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **time** | ✗(常) | 时间；次数 | I have no time today. |
| 2 | **year** | ✓ | 年 | I was born in 2000. |
| 3 | **way** | ✓ | 路；方法 | This is the best way to learn. |
| 4 | **day** | ✓ | 天 | See you in a few days. |
| 5 | **thing** | ✓ | 事情；东西 | Things are getting better. |
| 6 | **man / men** | ✓ | 男人 | He is an honest man. |
| 7 | **world** | ✓ | 世界 | She wants to see the world. |
| 8 | **life** | ✓/✗ | 生活；生命 | Life is full of surprises. |
| 9 | **work** | ✗ | 工作(不可数) | I have a lot of work today. |
| 10 | **school** | ✓ | 学校 | School starts at eight. |
| 11 | **friend** | ✓ | 朋友 | A friend in need is a friend indeed. |
| 12 | **money** | ✗ | 钱 | Money doesn't buy happiness. |

## 用法注意
- **work** 不可数：说 `a job`（一份工作），不说 `a work`。
- **money / time** 不可数：much time / much money；可数用 many。
- **friend 复数**：friends；注意发音 /frendz/。
