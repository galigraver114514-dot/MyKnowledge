# Unit 03 · 学习话题词

> 目标：能开口/落笔描述"你的学习安排"。本组均为学业与自我提升高频词。

| # | 单词 | 词性 | 核心义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **learn** | v. | 学习(技能/知识) | I learn English every day. |
| 2 | **study** | v./n. | 学习(系统性)；研究 | She studies at the library. |
| 3 | **practice** | v./n. | 练习 | Practice makes perfect. |
| 4 | **improve** | v. | 提高；改善 | I want to improve my listening. |
| 5 | **review** | v./n. | 复习；回顾 | Let's review Unit 1 together. |
| 6 | **goal** | n. | 目标 | My goal is 100 words this week. |
| 7 | **habit** | n. | 习惯 | Reading is a good habit. |
| 8 | **progress** | n. | 进步(不可数) | You've made great progress. |
| 9 | **note** | n./v. | 笔记；记录 | I take notes in class. |
| 10 | **test** | n./v. | 测试 | We have a test on Friday. |

## 用法注意
- **learn vs study**：learn 强调"学会"(结果)，study 强调"花时间学"(过程)：I studied hard and learned a lot.
- **progress 不可数**：make progress；不说 `a progress`。
- 口语链：set a goal → build a habit → make progress → review often。
