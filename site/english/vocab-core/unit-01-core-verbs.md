# Unit 01 · 高频动词 12

> 目标：见词 0.5s 内说出核心义 + 能造一个正确例句。完成标准：例句跟读流利 + 回译全对。

| # | 单词 | 词性 | 主题 | 核心义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **get** | v. | 获得·得到 | 得到；变得 | I get up at seven every day. |
| 2 | **make** | v. | 创造·制作 | 制作；使得 | She makes coffee for us. |
| 3 | **take** | v. | 携带·获取 | 拿；花费 | It takes me an hour to get there. |
| 4 | **know** | v. | 认知·思考 | 知道；认识 | Do you know the answer? |
| 5 | **think** | v. | 认知·思考 | 想；认为 | I think this plan works. |
| 6 | **come** | v. | 移动·来去 | 来 | He comes from Beijing. |
| 7 | **go** | v. | 移动·来去 | 去 | We go swimming on Sundays. |
| 8 | **look** | v. | 感官·视觉 | 看；看起来 | You look tired today. |
| 9 | **want** | v. | 意愿·想要 | 想要 | I want a cup of tea. |
| 10 | **use** | v. | 工具·使用 | 使用 | May I use your pen? |
| 11 | **find** | v. | 发现·寻找 | 找到；发觉 | I can't find my keys. |
| 12 | **give** | v. | 给予·传递 | 给 | Please give me a hand. |

## 用法注意
- **get** 口语极高频：get up / get to / get it（明白）。
- **make** 常见搭配：make a decision / make friends / make progress。
- **take** 记两个核心：take sth（拿）+ It takes ... to ...（花费）。
